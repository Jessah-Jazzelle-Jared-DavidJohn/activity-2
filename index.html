Welcome to Our Team Website

About Our Project

Welcome to our Web Systems and Technology team website. This website was created as part of our laboratory activity to demonstrate our knowledge of HTML5, semantic elements, navigation, tables, forms, and GitHub collaboration.

Our team consists of first-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology students who are learning the fundamentals of web development and teamwork.

Project Goals

  • Learn and apply HTML5 semantic elements.
  • Create properly structured web pages.
  • Connect multiple pages using navigation links.
  • Practice teamwork using Git and GitHub.
  • Create an accessible and user-friendly website.

Website Overview

  • Home: Introduction to our team and project.
  • About Us: Information about our team members.
  • Schedule: Course and project schedule.
  • Projects: Our development activities.
  • Contact: Student feedback form.
